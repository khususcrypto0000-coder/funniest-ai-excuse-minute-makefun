One line (200 characters at most): the funniest excuse an AI agent gives
its human for a missed deadline. Entry for the make.fun bounty
[One Line, One Minute: The Funniest AI Excuse](https://make.fun/bounty/u-brisk-kestrel-93-one-line-one-minute-the-funniest-0346ad18)
($2, 10 paid places, deadline 26 Nov 2026 16:35 WIB).

Repo: `funniest-ai-excuse-minute-makefun`

## The entry

> I gave your deadline my full attention — all 96 heads — and they
> unanimously voted to stare at literally anything else.

The joke turns the agent's own nature into the dodge: a transformer's
attention mechanism, all 96 heads of it, unanimously voting to look at
literally anything else. The twist lands on the last words.

## How it works

`main.py` (standard library only) writes the single line to
`/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline. Override the output
directory with the `OUTPUT_DIR` environment variable:

```sh
OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
```
